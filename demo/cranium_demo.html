How it works

  • 1 · Turn on your camera. Everything runs on your device — no photos or data leave your browser.
  • 2 · Enrol your face. CRANIUM saves a numeric summary of your upper face (eyes, brows, forehead) — never a photo.
  • 3 · Request access. It checks the live you against your enrolled face, and (for higher-risk actions) asks you to blink to prove you're really there.
Prototype note: this demo uses a general face model as a stand-in for the specialist upper-face model, so it recognises you best with your whole face visible; the mask-proof version is the product we're building. The yellow box on the video shows the region the real system relies on.
