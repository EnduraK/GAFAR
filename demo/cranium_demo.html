Runs entirely in your browser. Models load once from a public CDN (@vladmandic/face-api). CRANIUM is 1:1 verification only — a probe is compared against the single enrolled template, never searched against a database — the design choice that keeps it in the EU AI Act's verification carve-out. Best in Chrome/Edge/Safari over https:// or localhost (camera access requires a secure context).
