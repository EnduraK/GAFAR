# Biometric Data Consent Form — CRANIUM research pilot

> **Template, not legal advice.** A plain-English starting point for a small UK
> student research pilot. Biometric data is **special category** personal data
> under the **UK GDPR**, so the bar is high: explicit consent, a real DPIA, and
> minimal retention. Have the final wording checked by someone qualified — your
> university's data-protection / research-ethics contact at CU London is a
> sensible first stop — and complete the DPIA **before** you collect anything.

## 1. What we are asking you to do
We are testing whether software can recognise a person from the **upper part of the
face** (eyes, eyebrows, forehead, bridge of the nose). With your permission we will
record short webcam video and/or still images of your face, in these conditions:

- with your face uncovered;
- wearing a face mask that covers your nose and mouth;
- [optional: wearing glasses / other coverings];
- possibly on more than one camera or device.

The whole session takes about **[ __ ] minutes**.

## 2. What data we collect and create
- Images/short video of your face captured during the session.
- A **numeric "faceprint"** (a mathematical summary) computed from those images,
  used to test matching accuracy. This faceprint cannot be used to reconstruct a
  photograph of you.
- No name is required; you will be identified only by a **participant code**.

## 3. Why we collect it (purpose & lawful basis)
The **sole purpose** is to measure the accuracy of a research prototype. Under UK/EU
GDPR our lawful basis is your **explicit consent** (Article 9(2)(a)) for processing
special-category biometric data. We will **not** use your data for any other purpose,
and we will **not** use it to identify you in any live or operational system.

## 4. How it is stored and protected
- Stored on **[encrypted disk / access-controlled storage]**, accessible only to
  **[named researchers]**.
- Not stored in any public location and not used to train a commercial model without
  a separate, specific consent from you.

## 5. Sharing
Your data will **not be sold** and **not shared** with third parties, except
**[none / named processor, e.g. secure cloud storage]** acting on our instructions
under a data-processing agreement. It will not be transferred outside **[UK/EEA]**
without appropriate safeguards.

## 6. How long we keep it, and destruction
- We will keep your images and faceprint for no longer than **[e.g. 12 months]** from
  the date of collection, or until the research concludes, whichever is sooner.
- At that point the data will be **permanently deleted**.
- We will delete your data sooner if you ask us to (see section 7).

## 7. Your rights — you are in control
- Taking part is **voluntary**. You may **decline** or **stop at any time**, without
  giving a reason and without any penalty.
- You may **withdraw your consent at any time** and ask us to **delete all your data**
  by contacting **[email]**; we will confirm deletion.
- You have the right to **access** a copy of your data, to have it **corrected**, and
  (where applicable) to its **erasure** and **portability**.
- You may complain to the relevant supervisory authority (in the UK, the Information
  Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk).
